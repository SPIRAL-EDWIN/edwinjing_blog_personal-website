# EdwinOS blog homepage
This document scopes the October 29 redesign; EDWINOS_UI_ARCHITECTURE.md remains authoritative for shared components.

Home is a reading index with a real lab demonstration. Reading path: author and subject → browse notes → recent activity → three curated technical entry points.
Keep the same-origin handwritten heading and geometric reading stacks, 36px hero, existing section headings, 17px card titles, 15px intro, 14px list, 13px supporting text, 11–12px labels. Preserve both GitHub frame palettes and the exact footer.

Replace equal-weight pill CTAs with one primary Browse the notes action and plain secondary links. Portrait video has its intrinsic ratio, aligned to the right content grid. Use an asymmetric curated group, no duplicate card CTA, no internal ↗. Glass belongs on curated surfaces and small controls; structured grid belongs behind those surfaces and the intro, never behind article prose.

Motion supports state: hover plays real media, offscreen pauses it; press subtly compresses glass; opening is skippable and does not claim network progress. Continuous corners and SVG optics are progressive enhancements.
