# v3.9 homepage: figure legibility and video quilt

This is the local verification record for the 2026-11-03 homepage changes. No commit or deployment has been made for this round.

## Scope and source

The desktop hero places Collections beside the introduction and stacks it below
the introduction at narrow widths. The opening no longer waits for hero video.
Projects & Ideas retains the PowerPoint-derived BipedalWBC figure and adds an
“In motion” quilt beneath it; the figure SVG itself was not rebuilt or edited.

Edwin selected `z_workspace` and `orientation` for the two simulations, `lower`
for the z-height lab video, and the original board-wiping recording. All labels
use neutral descriptions, and the segments retain original speed. Private
originals, contact sheets, Drive identifiers, crop and segment details are
recorded in the nonpublished `experiments/home-redesign/media-source.md`.

## Final integration checks

- MkDocs strict build passed after integration. Python unittest discovery ran 23
  site tests (3 skipped) and 138 tool tests successfully. The figure-gate
  browser regression and the opening's deck, icon and typing checks passed.
- Edge headless at 375, 820 and 1440px in both palettes showed one, two and four
  columns respectively, no horizontal overflow and aligned quilt/figure edges.
- The four sources played; raster, mix and real states and reversal were checked
  visually. Save-Data requested no MP4; no-JavaScript and no-WebGL fallbacks
  displayed posters. Reduced motion loaded no video until explicit playback.
- Pausing persisted across five Home/Friends navigation cycles. Five old WebGL
  contexts were released and one remained for the current homepage. Offscreen
  clips paused and sufficiently distant sources detached.
- At 4× CPU throttling, an Edge DevTools trace measured 607 animation callbacks:
  0.78ms mean, 4.03ms P95, 8.59ms maximum. These include other page and test
  callbacks and are not complete per-frame measurements; the 4ms target cannot
  be claimed as met.
- The older `tests/test_figure_anchor_once.cjs` timed out waiting for two
  removed `.research-figure` diagrams. It predates their replacement by the
  BipedalWBC figure and is not a passing integration check. The current figure
  gate and resize regression passed separately.

Safari, a real touch device, the full requested viewport matrix, initial-load
transfer/CLS, pin geometry before and after loading, standalone rAF counts and
five cycles including an ENotes article were not verified. Source-code
reverse-engineering tooling was unavailable; the quilt implements the provided
reference description rather than claiming an independent bundle audit.
