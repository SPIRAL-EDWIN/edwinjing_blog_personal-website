# v3.8 BipedalWBC framework on the homepage

Built and verified in a Linux sandbox (MkDocs 1.6.1, Material 9.7.6, headless Chromium 156) on 2026-11-02. Not committed, pushed or deployed.

## Approach

Earlier rounds re-implemented PowerPoint's layout (text line model, arrowheads, image resampling) and never converged. This round starts from **PowerPoint's own PDF export** of `Origin_framework.pptx` (`pdftocairo -svg`), so every glyph outline, glyph origin, outline, arrowhead and composited image layer is PowerPoint's. `tools/build_bipedalwbc_figure.py` only regroups the 246 primitives into the 60 OOXML leaf elements, in OOXML paint order. Two deliberate substitutions: the 12 math labels use the PPTX MathJax vector strips with their `srcRect` (sharper than PowerPoint's 300 ppi raster), and "Delopyment" is respelled with PowerPoint's own glyphs and advances.

## Limitations

- WebKit/Safari and Firefox were not tested (Chromium only).
- Real touch devices were not tested; touch was emulated.
- In the narrow-screen step views, the smallest 10 pt labels render at about 7.6 px at 375 px wide (6.1 px at 320). The full figure above them is legible only when zoomed.
- `tests/test_bipedalwbc_connector_freeze.py`'s optional `FIGURE_SVG` mode targets the v3.6 group format; the new `tests/test_bipedalwbc_figure.py` freezes the same vertices for the shipped SVG.
- The artwork keeps its paper colours on a white plate in dark mode.
