# Visual thesis

A working lab notebook becomes a quiet, luminous stage where real hardware and layered research diagrams make ideas tangible.

- A question surfaces from the grid, resolves, and gives way to the notebook.
- Research figures reveal relationships in reading order as the reader scrolls, with a complete static alternative.
- Real robot footage stands on its own as an invitation to explore the work.
